Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common BMW dealership questions

Can I schedule a test drive?

Yes. Visit our contact page or call the dealership to schedule a test drive for any available model.

Do you offer financing?

Our dealership offers financing options for qualified buyers. A team member can explain available plans and requirements.

Can I trade in my current vehicle?

Yes. Bring your vehicle and ownership documents to receive an appraisal and discuss trade-in options.

Do you sell electric BMW models?

We offer electric and hybrid BMW vehicles, including models in the BMW i lineup, subject to availability.

How can I check model availability?

Review the models page or contact us directly for the latest inventory and arrival information.
